    Sensitivity matches 1:1 only with raw input, which Chrome and Edge give to pointer-locked pages. Other browsers apply your OS pointer speed; set Windows pointer speed to 6/11 and turn off Enhance pointer precision. The mouse readouts update after a few seconds of play. On macOS, browsers receive cursor points rather than raw counts: if you need a very high sensitivity here, raise macOS tracking speed and your mouse DPI, then lower this sensitivity.
